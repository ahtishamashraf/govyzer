## Known limitations

- **TOTP multi-factor authentication is not implemented.** The schema and session
  model are MFA-ready, but no enrolment or challenge flow exists. The build prompt
  made TOTP optional; it is called out here rather than left implied.
- Provider-specific portal transports are stubbed at the transport layer only and
  report `awaiting_provider_credentials`. They are not labelled as live integrations
  anywhere in the product or the docs.
- Document templates carry no legal wording and are not represented as legally
  approved text.
